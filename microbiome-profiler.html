Tacrolimus Consumption by Gut Bacteria
Genomic Depth · 8 High-Metabolizing Species Profiled
Methodology: Drug consumption values represent % consumed from published datasets (Zimmermann et al., 2019 & Zimmermann et al., 2019 Cell). Gene catalogs extracted from NCBI genome assemblies for 8 bacterial species showing highest drug-metabolizing capacity. High-risk threshold (>50% consumption) indicates potential for clinically significant pharmacokinetic variability. Analysis performed as part of graduate research in the Bhushan Lab, IIT Chicago.

Clinical Relevance: Tacrolimus is a narrow therapeutic index immunosuppressant — microbiome-driven consumption variability directly affects systemic drug exposure and toxicity risk. Sulfasalazine undergoes azo-reduction by gut bacteria, producing active metabolites. Interpersonal variation in gut microbiome composition may explain observed drug response heterogeneity.
